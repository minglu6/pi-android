MesloLGLDZ Nerd Font Mono (Regular + Bold) for pi-android.

Variant: Meslo LG Large, Dotted Zero (DZ), Nerd Font Mono patch
  — MesloLGLDZNerdFontMono-Regular.ttf / -Bold.ttf from Meslo.zip

trim APK; CJK falls back to system sans-serif via AppFonts.
